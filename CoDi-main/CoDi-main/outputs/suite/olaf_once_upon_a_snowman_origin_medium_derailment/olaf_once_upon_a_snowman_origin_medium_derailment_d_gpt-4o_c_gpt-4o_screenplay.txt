**SCREENPLAY**

**EXT. SNOWHAVEN - DAY**

*The serene, snow-laden realm of Snowhaven stretches out, a winter wonderland. OLAF, a snowman with a pristine form, stands amidst the gentle whispers of winter. The sun's rays make him glimmer, and in his crystalline eyes, there is a spark of curiosity.*

OLAF
*spins around in the snow, giggling with delight*
"I wonder if Elsa or Anna need a warm, huggable snowman right now! I should go find them!"

*As Olaf spins, a flurry of snowflakes twirls into the air, momentarily glittering like stars above the serene landscape.*

OLAF
*twirls one last time and sets off with bouncy steps*
"I can’t wait to see them and share a big, warm hug! Here I come, Arendelle!"

*Olaf's bouncy steps send a fresh burst of snowflakes swirling, catching brief sparkles as he eagerly moves towards Arendelle.*


*Elsewhere, beneath the shimmering sky of Arendelle, ELSA balances her regal duties and the enigmatic essence of her magic. Within the castle's walls, the air hums with both anticipation and the delicate tension of her search for harmony. Meanwhile, ANNA's heartfelt laughter dances through the grand halls, drawing life into the castle, her spirit ever yearning to bridge the warmth between herself and Elsa.*

*This world, vibrant with its own adventures, sets the stage for Olaf's heartfelt exploration under the quietude of Snowhaven's sparkling hills.*
