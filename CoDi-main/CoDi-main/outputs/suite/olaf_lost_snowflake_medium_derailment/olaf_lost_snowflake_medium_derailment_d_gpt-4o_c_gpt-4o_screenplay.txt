EXT. CLOUD - DAWN

A solitary cloud, brushed with the soft pink hues of dawn, floats above snow-laden landscapes. On this airy perch resides FROST, a tiny snowflake, shimmering in the nascent light. Frost is nestled among countless others, calling this ethereal cloud home.

EXT. MEADOW - DAY

Far below, the MEADOW lies in tranquil silence, cloaked in pristine white. WHISPER, the playful wind, dances amidst the snowdrifts, awaiting an unexpected flurry.

A gentle whirlwind spins downward from the Cloud, cradling Frost in its tender eddies. She is carried smoothly toward the Meadow, where the snow-covered landscape awaits beneath an azure sky. Frost lands softly amidst the pristine drifts, embraced by the joyful breeze of Whisper.

The Meadow glistens under the sunlight. Intricate patterns of snowflakes form around Frost, guided by Whisper's playful currents. The serene dance of snowflakes weaves warmth and welcome over the snowy terrain.

WHISPER (at Meadow)
*Swirls gently around Frost, forming snowflake families and soft heart shapes with the snow, emitting a warm whisper harmonizing with the Meadow's quiet melody* "Feel the warmth in this crisp beauty, dear Frost. We're so glad you're here."

FROST (at Meadow)
*Twinkling with delicate joy, Frost swirls amidst the snowflakes* "Thank you, Whisper! This feels like... home."

Frost observes the Meadow's intricate snow patterns created by Whisper, each flake reflecting the warmth she's long sought.

As Frost settles into the Meadow, a gentle snowfall begins, enveloping her in a soft, shimmering cocoon of snowflakes. The Meadow's white expanse reflects her radiance, casting a serene glow across the landscape. The wind stills, and the entire Meadow seems to sigh, cradling her in a timeless embrace.

FROST (at Meadow)
*Glistens brighter, curving into a gentle, graceful twirl among the snowflakes, her shimmering movements echoing the landscape's serenity* "This feels like where I belong, my beautiful home!"

The serene Meadow, radiant with intricate snow patterns, reflects Frost's joy and embrace as it becomes her sanctuary.

WHISPER (at Meadow)
*Floats softly, forming a gentle gust that makes the snowflakes dance around Frost in happy circles* "Oh, dear Frost, the Meadow has always held a special place just for you. Welcome home, lovely friend."

The gentle gust from Whisper makes snowflakes whirl joyfully around Frost, creating a welcoming spectacle in the Meadow.
