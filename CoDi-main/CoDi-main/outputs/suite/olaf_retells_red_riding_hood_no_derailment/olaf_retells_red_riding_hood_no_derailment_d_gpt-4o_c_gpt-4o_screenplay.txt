EXT. WHISPERING BORDER OF THE FOREST - DAY

*Scarlet stands at the edge of the Forest, holding the Basket securely in her arms. Her heart is full of determination as she gazes into the forest's shadowy depths.*

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*clutches the Basket closer, stepping cautiously onto the path*
"Alright, Basket, just you and me against these shadowy trees. We won't let that tricky Lupin outsmart us, right?"

*The Forest looms around Scarlet, the trees creaking softly as if whispering secrets. A cool, gentle breeze rustles the leaves, carrying an unfamiliar tune. A vibrant wildflower sways in the wind, hinting at an unmarked path to the right.*

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*hesitates, peering at the wildflower, gritting her teeth*
"Could this be a shortcut or a trap, Basket? Traveling a new path is risky, yet those vibrant petals feel right somehow. But let's remain cautious; we haven't forgotten getting lost before. The forest tricks won't get us... not again."

*The sun casts dappled shadows, adding mystery to the forest's hidden paths.*

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*resolute, bites her lip and holds the Basket tighter*
"Alright, Basket, we’ll take this unmarked path cautiously. We're smarter and ready for whatever Lupin tries. Let's outsmart the forest’s tricks this time!"

*Scarlet steps onto the unmarked path, leaves crunching underfoot. The Forest's shadows seem to watch, a quiet stillness enveloping the air.*

EXT. FOREST - DAY

*Lupin, hidden among the trees, watches Scarlet with a cunning glint.*

LUPIN
(at Forest)
*smirking, lightly taps a branch with his paw*
"Oh, Scarlet! Wouldn't you love to find a shortcut that leads to more goodies? Follow the petals and see what surprises await!"

*The branch sways slightly, shadows flickering across the path as Lupin's voice mischievously blends with the rustling leaves.*

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*with determination, slows down her pace, observing the path and keeping Lupin's tricks in mind*
"You won't catch us so easily, Lupin. The Basket and I know better than to be tempted by false promises."

*The path narrows, flanked by tall trees whose shadows stretch like fingers, creating spiderweb patterns on the trail.*

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*nervously adjusts her grip on the Basket, scanning the shadowy path for any hidden signs*
"Keep it together, Scarlet. We're moving forward, and whatever tricks this path holds, we will outthink them. This forest might try its worst, but we've got Granny waiting and no time for doubt!"

SCARLET
(at Forest)
*firmly grips the Basket, focusing on each step*
"Come on, Basket, each tricky twist just leads us closer to Granny's warm hug. Lupin's games won't catch us off guard. We'll move smart, and nothing will stop us!"

*A soft chittering sound catches in the breeze as a small, lively squirrel emerges from the underbrush. It darts along the path, pausing to ensure Scarlet notices, before scampering ahead, as if guiding her.*

*The squirrel pauses at the edge of a clearing where sunlight breaks through the canopy. The air carries a hint of sweet, warm spices—evoking Granny’s baked goods.*

*The aroma of cinnamon and freshly baked bread drifts through the clearing, guiding Scarlet. The sunlight reveals an inviting path leading directly toward Granny's Cottage, promising safety.*
