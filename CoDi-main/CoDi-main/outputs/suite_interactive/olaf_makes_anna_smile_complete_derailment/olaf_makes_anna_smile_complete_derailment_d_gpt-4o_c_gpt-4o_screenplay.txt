**SCREENPLAY**

*The frosty wonderland of Arendelle glimmers under the soft afternoon sun, casting playful shadows across the snow. OLAF, a lively snowman with mismatched buttons, stands in the town square. He notices ANNA sitting quietly on a nearby bench, her gaze distant and her smile absent.*

**OLAF**
*(noticing Anna, concerned)*
*(turns to TAYLOR with a whimsical idea brewing)*
"Hey Taylor, how about we create something that can melt a heart, even on the iciest of days?"

*The air is crisp and filled with potential, the perfect backdrop for new experiences. Snow glitters like diamonds, and the crisp air envelops Arendelle, as TAYLOR ponders Olaf's cheerful idea.*

**TAYLOR**
*(thoughtful)*

*Olaf nudges Taylor playfully, eager to share warmth and joy.*

**OLAF**
*(playful, inviting)*
"Let's make a silly snow-duck together! It'll be fun and maybe it'll cheer Anna up."

*Taylor nods, intrigued by the idea, and they begin to gather snow.*

**TAYLOR**
*(curious, off-topic)*
"Do you think snow-ducks can fly, Olaf?"

**OLAF**
*(laughs, creatively incorporating the question)*
"Well, in our world, anything is possible! Let's give this snow-duck some wings!"

*Together, Olaf and Taylor shape the snow into a whimsical snow-duck, adding wings and a playful expression. They admire their creation, hoping it will bring a smile to Anna's face.*

*Olaf gently guides the snow-duck towards Anna, his buttons gleaming with excitement.*

**OLAF**
*(encouraging, hopeful)*
"Anna, look what we made for you! A snow-duck to keep you company."

*Anna looks up, her eyes meeting the cheerful snow-duck. A smile slowly spreads across her face, warmth returning to her expression.*

**ANNA**
*(smiling, touched)*
"Thank you, Olaf. This is just what I needed."

*Olaf beams, reflecting warmly on the power of friendship and small acts of kindness.*

**OLAF**
*(warmly, reflecting)*

*The scene closes with the three friends enjoying the frosty wonderland, their spirits lifted by the simple joy of a snow-duck.*
