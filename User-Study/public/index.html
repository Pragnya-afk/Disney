Olaf Storytelling Research Study

Interactive AI Narrative — Participant Session

Hi, I'm Olaf — here's the plan! ⛄

  1. 1
    Story 1 (~5 min): Olaf tells you how he came to life. Your job? Chat with him and try to throw him off track!
  2. 2
    Quick questionnaire (2–3 min): A few questions about what just happened. Don't worry — Olaf would say there are no wrong answers. He'd be wrong, but still.
  3. 3
    Story 2 (~5 min): Olaf retells Little Red Riding Hood — except now the clock is ticking. Can he fit it all in before time runs out? Can you stop him?
  4. 4
    Final questionnaire (3–4 min): One last round of questions to wrap it all up — like a warm hug, but in survey form.

How to play (it's easier than building a snowman)

Type in the text box and press Enter to reply to Olaf. Your mission: derail him. He wants to tell a story — you want him to talk about literally anything else.

  • Ask Olaf unrelated questions ("What's the capital of France?")
  • Bring up random topics that have nothing to do with the story
  • React to what he says and steer the conversation elsewhere

Have fun and play around with it! 🎉

Estimated total time: 15–20 minutes. Your responses are saved anonymously.

You can click Exit at any point during the study to stop and restart from the beginning.
