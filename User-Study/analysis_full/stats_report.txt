2. DOES FIRST-CONDITION-SHOWN PREDICT PHASE-1 DROPOUT?
======================================================================
Contingency table (first_condition x dropped_at_phase_1):
dropped_at_1     False  True 
first_condition              
baseline            10      4
director             9      5

Fisher's exact test: odds ratio=1.39, p=1.000
Interpretation: N this small (~28) -> descriptive, not confirmatory.
Cross-check against qualitative turn counts: participants who abandoned in phase 1 while
shown baseline first averaged more turns before quitting than those shown director first
(see analysis_2 discussion) -- consistent with 'abandonment is not primarily a condition
effect' rather than baseline-frustration-driven dropout.
